Prototype controls

Not part of the extension — these let you time-travel and switch sites. Hover and click the dots in the headings.

  • Click the orange dots (today's letter) to “spot” them all
  • Click any other cell to peek at the print letter
  • Open ⚙ Settings in the popup: level, which headings, letter order
  • Drag to days 27–31 for the end-of-month specials
  • Snooze, then resume
